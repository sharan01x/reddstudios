A web app to help you keep up with on-going lawsuits. Clients can ask any question about their case without taking up a lawyer's time, while lawyers get instant access to case details — people, timelines and facts — without wading through thousands of pages. Powered by RAG over case documents with AI-assisted discussion, timeline extraction and actor identification.
